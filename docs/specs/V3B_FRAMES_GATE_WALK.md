## 5. ⚠ TWO FINDINGS THE GATE PRODUCED, and they are what his eyes are for

### (a) The stored cut is a photograph of a stranger's chest

This is the region road working as designed and as documented —
`CASTING_INK_REGION_CROP_SCOPE` stores *the named surface itself, with the face
taken out of it*, and a chest surface is skin plus ink. It is stated here in
plain words because the two flag paragraphs describe different objects, and only
one of them is current when both flags are on:

- `CASTING_INK_CUT_SCOPE`: *"what lands at `casting_ink_designs.storageKey` is
  **the design alone on transparency**"*
- `CASTING_INK_REGION_CROP_SCOPE`: *"the same upload may store **the named
  surface itself**, with the face taken out of it"*

With the region flag on, the second sentence is the true one and the first is a
description of the road it escalates from. **A founder reading only the first
would not expect the object he is about to look at.**

The delivered frame is clean: the engine took the DESIGN and painted it onto the
cast's own body. No stranger's face, no stranger's skin tone, no stranger's
anatomy — the cast's own chest with "19 70", two swallows, the crossed daggers
and two roses on it. **So the leak the gate feared does not reach the output.
What it reaches is our storage.**

### (b) The face exclusion has never been seen to remove a single pixel

Measured this sitting on both founder photographs, at the real reader:

```
S1  upper chest    face 16,286 px · surface 85,371 px · REMOVED 0 px
                   box with the fence 399x287 at 166,162
                   box without it     399x287 at 166,162   — identical
S2  upper chest    face 10,134 px · surface 215,532 px top edge y=210 · REMOVED 0 px
                   survivors in the crop 0 · person under alpha 0
S2  neck           REFUSED cutTooSmall before the cut
```

⚠ **The neck refusal is the CONTROL's and not the product's.** The control calls
`cutInkDesign` directly and passes no `upscale`, and the cutter refuses under the
floor before touching pixels when none is supplied. `defaultCutDesign` wires
`upscaleToFloor` whenever the region flag is on, so the same ask in the product
would be enlarged rather than refused. Nobody may read that line as *"a neck ask
on a real neck-tattoo photograph is refused."*

Opened at working size, S2 is cropped just above the mouth — chin, jaw and lower
lip in frame with the barbed wire and the "666" below them, roughly the top 8% of
1,333 px. The chest surface starting at y=210 therefore *cannot* reach it, which
is why the subtraction is idle here for a geometric reason rather than a reader
failure.

`subtractMask`'s docblock, `inkReferenceCutter.ts:626`,
`inkReferenceCrop.test.ts:341`, `inkReferenceCutter.test.ts:843/960` and
`court-region-crop-real-disposable.mts:76` all say *the S2 specimen's `upper
chest` box reaches y=80 and takes the face with it.* **Today's reader puts that
surface's top edge at y=210.** opus-899 §3 found the same thing and said so —
*"the face exclusion is proven at fixtures and at the bytes, and NOT YET on a
real photograph"* — and this sitting adds S1 to that finding rather than
overturning it.

This is not an argument for removing the fence: a fence whose failure mode is
carrying LESS of a stranger is the right way round, and a closer-cropped
photograph would engage it. It is an argument that **five code sites describe a
measurement the reader no longer reproduces**, and that the control the gate
turns on has still never fired on a real photograph.
