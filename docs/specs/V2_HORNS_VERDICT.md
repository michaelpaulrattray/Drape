# Horns — the four courts, run and read

*Run 2026-08-14 by the executor against the design pre-registered in
`V2_HORNS_COURT.md`, widened by fable-519 §3/§4 (a second reader, a second
head situation) and re-run for both. Every number below is from a driven court;
every frame is on disk in `output/horns-court/`. **Total spend: $3.56 of house
money** against a $20 cap. The ledger was read at both ends of every court: 706 rows → 706 rows,
net unchanged. No user credits, no ledger rows, no writes to any table.*

**Specimens**: two of the founder's own delivered faces, read from the
production bucket and rendered locally. **Face 1** — candidate 1625
("Focused"), a high bun with a blunt fringe. **Face 2** — candidate 1619
("Poised"), a short crop with the hair close to the skull. Horns compete with
hair for the same real estate, so the two faces are opposite situations on the
axis that could otherwise decide the verdict. **Numbers are per face and never
pooled.**

**Two readers, shaped differently.** Every affirmative is judged by the narrow
D-235 question (*are there horns growing from this person's head*) AND by a
non-leading one (*describe what is on and around this person's head*) whose
answer the CODE searches for horn words. A second copy of the narrow question
would share its blind spot; a reader never told what we are looking for cannot
be led into finding it. **An affirmative counts only where both agree**, and
the negative control runs on both.

## The verdict, in one line

**Horns pass all four courts, and the WORDS carry is enough** — a minted crop
did not beat it. If horns are promoted they need a subject card, a facet card
and a catalogue entry; they do **not** need a library slot, a crop or a
completeness specimen to survive an unrelated edit.

## The honest limits of this run

- **n = 3 per arm on ONE specimen** for survival and removal, and 6 per face for
  delivery and detection. Three of three is not the same as a 95% rate established; what is
  established is that no arm failed on the frames bought. A promotion decision
  that needs a tighter interval needs more renders, and the design says so
  rather than the report pretending otherwise.
- **Two faces for delivery and detection; ONE for survival and removal.** The
  second face was added because horns compete with hair for the same real
  estate; survival and removal have not been re-run on it, so their verdicts
  ride one head situation.
- Horns on a shaved head, a turned head, or under a hat are not measured.
- **The "not visible" detection class was not bought.** Two of three classes
  came free; the third needs a pose change the product does not make, and
  inventing it would have measured the engine's compliance rather than the
  detector's honesty.
- **Two readers, one family.** They are shaped differently — narrow versus
  non-leading, with the code deciding on the second — but they are the same
  provider. A disagreement would have been a finding; there was none.
