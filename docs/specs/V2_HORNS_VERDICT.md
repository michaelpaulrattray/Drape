## The honest limits of this run

- **n = 3 per arm on ONE specimen** for survival and removal, and 6 for
  delivery. Three of three is not the same as a 95% rate established; what is
  established is that no arm failed on the frames bought. A promotion decision
  that needs a tighter interval needs more renders, and the design says so
  rather than the report pretending otherwise.
- **One face.** A blonde woman with a fringe and a bun. Horns on a shaved head,
  on a turned head, or under a hat are not measured.
- **The "not visible" detection class was not bought.** Two of three classes
  came free; the third needs a pose change the product does not make, and
  inventing it would have measured the engine's compliance rather than the
  detector's honesty.
- **The judge is one reader.** Its two controls hold in both directions on
  these frames, which is what makes its verdicts count here; it is not a
  general-purpose horn detector.
