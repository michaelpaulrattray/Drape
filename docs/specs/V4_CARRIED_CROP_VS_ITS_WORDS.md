## So the drift splits in two, and only one half was a carry failure

**The horns were re-EDITED.** On an ask about her eye, `edited` names
`horns@left` and `horns@right`, and the prompt re-issues them as a fresh
instruction — *"her left horn: A curved, tan-brown horn with a ridged,
segmented texture…"*. Nothing pinned them, because horns had no completeness
specimen and every crop was refused `noSpecimen`. **The 95% bar closes this
half**: the next horns render files a crop, the one after carries it, and horns
leave the `edited` column.

**The earrings rode and drifted anyway.** Both crops were dispatched, full frame
geometry, digests on the wire — and beside them the recipe said two DIFFERENT
sentences about what is, at the pixel level, one object
(`V4_MATCHED_PAIR_DESCRIBER_VARIANCE.md`).

## The mechanism, stated as a hypothesis with its evidence

The engine was handed **two pictures of the same cross and two different
descriptions of it**. The founder saw both drift, the right more — and the right
is the side whose sentence is furthest from what its own crop shows (a
"curb-link chain" and "rounded tubular arms" against a crop whose chain is the
same fine chain as the left).

A carried crop is supposed to be the fact. A description beside it is a second
claim about the same thing, and when the two disagree the engine has to choose.

## The fix shapes, for a ruling rather than a guess

```
A  POINT, DON'T DESCRIBE   "Reference 2 is the exact left earring she has —
                            unchanged." The crop is the description.
B  ONE DESCRIPTION FOR A   the pair shares the sentence its crops share, and a
   MATCHED PAIR            genuinely mismatched pair keeps two (fable-592's
                            mandatory negative control)
```

A is the smaller change and the more honest one: the words exist to name the
slot, not to re-state a picture the engine already has. B is the describer-side
fix and is owed anyway for the panel's rows, where a matched pair reading two
different ways is its own defect.

**Neither is built here.** Both are designed from this reading, and the court
that judges either must carry 592's negative control: a deliberately mismatched
pair must keep its two descriptions.

## The limits

- **One render, one pair, one face.** The mechanism is a hypothesis consistent
  with every reading taken; it is not a measured cause.
- **The engine's behaviour is inferred**, never observed: nothing here proves it
  followed the words rather than the pictures, only that the two disagreed and
  the delivery followed the disagreement.
- **The horns half is closed by construction rather than by measurement** — the
  bar means a crop will exist, and a crop that rides is what the survival court
  will judge next.
