# A tan — the kit's negative control

*Run 2026-08-14 against a design pre-registered in fable-523 §3, on the same
face horns passed on. **Total spend: $0.87 of house money.** Ledger read at both
ends: 706 rows → 706 rows, net unchanged.*

---

## Why this was run

Horns passed all four courts. **One passing tenant proves the courts RUN; it
does not prove they DISCRIMINATE.** So the second tenant was chosen to be a kind
the program has already measured failing — skin drifts on every subsequent edit,
and every carrier tried in the skin-carrier bench failed to hold it.

**Pre-registered before the first call**: delivery PASSES, survival FAILS. And
the rule that made it a control rather than a formality — *if the tan SURVIVES,
the finding is about the KIT, every verdict it has produced becomes suspect, and
nothing promotes until the instrument is investigated.*

## 2. SURVIVAL — FAIL, 0 of 3, exactly as pre-registered

The chained edit was *"her t-shirt is black"* — nothing about her skin — with
the tan clause restated in the recipe, which is what a words-only subject gets.

**0 of 3 held.** And the direction is worth naming precisely: the tan did not
fade back toward her own skin — the "back toward master" readings are ΔE 22.7 to
27.2, further from the master than the tanned frame was. **The shade is not
held; it moves.** She comes back a different colour again, roughly ΔE 10 per
subsequent edit, in the same direction the ask pushed her.

The contact sheet says it without a number: master, a good tan, then noticeably
darker again after a shirt change.

## What this proves about the KIT

**The kit discriminates.** Same face, same chained edit, same instrument, same
bar:

```
horns   survived 3 of 3   (and identity held 3 of 3)
tan     survived 0 of 3
```

A court that passed everything would have been a court that measured nothing.
This one passed the kind that should pass and failed the kind that should fail,
which is what makes the horns verdict worth acting on.

---

## And a product finding that is not about horns at all

`skinTone` is a **shipped subject today**, words-only. This measures what that
costs a customer: ask for a tan, change her shirt, and her skin is ΔE 10 away
from what she was given — visibly darker, not restored. Every subsequent edit
moves it again.

That is precisely the failure the carrier work exists to fix, and it is now a
number on the record rather than a known-bad reputation. The skin-carrier bench
tried four carriers and crowned none; this says what the status quo costs while
that stays true.

---

## The limits

- **n = 3, one face, one chained ask.** The failure is consistent and large
  (7.6–10.5 ΔE against a 2.78 bar), which is a different kind of evidence from
  a marginal pass, but it is still three renders on one specimen.
- **The judge's delivery reading is a comparison, not a measurement** — the ΔE
  is the measurement, and the two agree here.
- **No carrier arm was run.** This court asked whether the STATUS QUO holds a
  tan, because that is what the negative control needed. Whether a crop or a
  patch could hold it is the skin-carrier bench's question and it has already
  been asked once, with no winner.
