## 2. What unites them — the taste being pointed at

1. **They are characters, not models.** Every image reads as someone with a
   story — a chieftain, a priestess, a damaged machine — where our sheets
   deliver castable *people*. The gap is identity density, not rendering
   quality; several are no more "detailed" than our frames, they are more
   *specific*.
2. **Category is the engine of the quality.** Each image commits totally to
   what the subject IS — orc, android, dragon-kin — and every surface
   follows from that commitment (tusks push the lip line, chrome meets torn
   skin at a believable seam, scale wraps the brow). This is the CASTING
   CATEGORY block's job description, at maximum strength.
3. **Half of them are hybrids on a human armature** (elf, orc, android,
   cyber-fashion) — human face and body logic carrying non-human features.
   The other half abandon the human armature entirely (quadruped, bone
   horror). These are DIFFERENT capability classes for us (see §4).
4. **Surface materials go beyond skin**: chrome, latex, scale, bone, fur,
   filigree metal. Our reader vocabulary is built on `human skin`, `face`,
   `upper arm` — measured words, measured on humans.
5. **At least two are recognizable franchise characters** (Kaguya; a
   Roshi-type elder; the orc is Warcraft-adjacent). The product's
   `unsupported_cohort` wall exists exactly here (the founder's own
   "inspired by goku" test met it). **"That quality/type" must mean original
   creations at that craft level — not those characters.** This is a stance
   the founder should ratify explicitly when this program starts.

## 3. Where the current system already points at this

- **The role/category field** (`intent.role` → CASTING CATEGORY block) is
  the spine. The court of 2026-08-25 proved category presence is what buys
  in-category inventiveness; the re-ask made it reliable. Creative casts are
  category commitment taken from "cybernetic augmented man" to "pale orc
  chieftain."
- **The settings modal** (founder-designed, six rulings, fable-1546/1547/1548)
  already reserves the front door: *cast style* as a setting, with lighting /

  selector.** Nothing about the modal design needs to change shape.
- **The basics-default wardrobe direction** (fable-1643) already rules that
  basics are category-suited and *"a monster might not even wear anything"*
  — the wardrobe layer was designed with this future in it.
- **The open lane** proved the grammar for out-of-vocabulary kinds on
  edits; creative cohorts are the same philosophy at birth.
- **The born-ink lane** proved briefs can carry structured non-core facts
  (described markings) into the library. Scales/plating/decals are the same

## 4. What actually breaks — the honest capability map

The roll is the EASY half. Everything downstream assumes a human:

| Surface | Human assumption | On a creature/android |
|---|---|---|
| Interpreter axes | sex/age/heritage/build | heritage is meaningless for an android; axes must be per-cohort |
| Variance spread | heritage/build/hair/greying | needs cohort-declared axes (silhouette, material, palette, ornament) |
| Face panel / scan | 12 human feature questions | segmenter words unmeasured on scale/chrome; rows may be nonsense |
| Ink studio | human placement vocabulary | "upper arm" on a quadruped: undefined |
| Sign views | 6 canonical human views, conformance reader | view conformance calibrated on humans only |
| Wardrobe/VTO | human garments | category basics (ruled); VTO on non-human bodies is its own program |
| Refusal walls | fantastical anatomy → wall | the wall IS the current behaviour; opening it is the feature |
| Engine safety | human portraits mostly clean | gore-adjacent horror and skin-adjacent android bodies raise refusal rates — measure per cohort (the basics refusal-court discipline, fable-1645) |

**The class distinction that sizes the work:** hybrids-on-human-armature
(elf/orc/android/cyber-fashion) inherit most of the human pipeline — faces
scan, ink places, views conform, garments fit. Non-humanoid creatures
inherit almost none of it. **Phase the program on that line.**

## 5. Proposed shape (for design later, not now)

1. **Cohort modules, not prompt forks**: `cohortPhotorealHuman.ts` becomes
   one of N cohort modules; each declares its category grammar, its variance
   axes, its basics, its refusal walls, and — critically — its **capability
   manifest** (which downstream doors are honest for this cohort). The
   capability atlas already knows how to hold doors; a cohort ships with its
   doors declared or walled, never silently broken.
2. **Phase A — "heroic human-adjacent"** (elf/orc/android/cyber-fashion):
   human armature + category commitment + per-cohort axes. Full pipeline
   mostly works; refine/sign court per cohort before opening. This phase
   alone covers 9 of his 13 references.
3. **Phase B — creatures off the human armature**: roll + sheet first,
   refine/sign honestly walled per the atlas discipline, capabilities
   opened one court at a time. (The grey mutant on a seamless backdrop shows
   even monsters can live in sheet framing.)
4. **The ratchet applies unchanged**: build dark behind a scope flag per
   cohort, dogfood on the founder's account, court the walls and refusal
   rates, his eyes gate, widen. Identical to ink/two-paths/fidelity.
5. **Originality stance**: named/franchise characters stay walled
   (existing behaviour, founder-tested); the product's promise is "an orc
   chieftain of your own," never "that orc." Needs founder ratification at
   program start.
