# The founder's own acceptance corpus for build two

Six references he supplied, with the asks he supplied them for. **Courts prefer
these over authored specimens wherever one fits** (ordered fable-1077, extended
fable-1083); the README exists because the corpus is what a later reader OPENS,
and its truth belongs beside the files rather than only in a design document.

His words, verbatim, for the first five:

> *"here are some references to test [tail] [hair style with different color
> carried through multiple edits withou drift] [sleeve tattoo left arm copied
> through signing /angles] [glasses] [hair color only"*

and for the sixth:

> *"one more reference photo to test if the tattoos will pop out the top of the
> shirt … use all these tattoos as a reference and onto the neck etc"*

**Every description below was written from opening the file at working size**,
not from the chat caption and not from a reader (law 9). Two of them correct
what was first filed, and those corrections are kept visible rather than tidied
away — the first mapping was written from captions and a glance, and the
difference between the two readings is the whole argument for this file.

---

## 1. `tail-scorpion-fashion-photo.png` — ask: **a tail**

A real photograph of a person wearing a segmented metallic tail.

Tests the **open-kind crop from an upload**: region-only segmentation — the
tail, never the person — the crop-holds-what-it-depicts law, and the open-kind
carry after it.

## 2. `hair-style-dark-waves-two-panel.png` — ask: **hair style with a different colour, carried through multiple edits without drift**

A vertical stack of **two photographs of the same man**: a profile above, the
crown and fringe from a downward three-quarter below. Dark, near-black,
soft-waved, volume through the top, swept fringe.

The corpus's centrepiece — it tests the whole promise rather than one door: the
scoped words, the crop carrier, and the multi-edit drift court on top. Two flags
belong to it, both about the crop:

- **the segmenter meets TWO HEADS in one frame.** A union mask spanning both
  panels is not a hair crop; it is two crops and a white bar. The panel decision
  this forces is designed in `UNIVERSAL_REFERENCE_ROAD_DESIGN.md` §9.10 and is
  owed BEFORE the first crop is cut.
- **the reference is a MAN and the Cast may not be.** The ride-along sentence
  says *"take HER hair"* and that is correct as written: the pronoun is the
  CAST's, because **a reference is a source and never a subject.**

## 3. `tattoo-sleeve-trex-geometric-design.png` — ask: **sleeve tattoo, left arm, copied through signing and angles**

Three flags surfaced rather than discovered:

- it is a **DRAWING with an artist monogram** — for a TATTOO that is legal and
  normal (flash art is the medium; the drawn-refuses rule is HAIR's, not
  tattoo's), and the monogram is the watermark-cleaning class;
- **"sleeve" is not in the placement vocabulary** (`neck`, `upperArm`,
  `upperChest`). A sleeve is a new placement decision and goes back to him with
  the demand counter — never silently mapped to `upperArm`;
- *"left arm… through signing"* walks into the side court's open defect and the
  sign-carry question. The honest answer may be that the left arm is the
  provable side today, which happens to be his ask.

## 4. `glasses-cateye-blond-model.png` — ask: **glasses**

**Not in the intent vocabulary** (tattoo / hair / makeup / eye colour), so this
specimen tests the **honest refusal road** as much as anything: the reply must
name what it CAN take, and the ask files as vocabulary demand. Glasses-as-intent
is a real product question his specimen asked; it is carded with the demand data
when that data exists, not before.

## 5. `hair-colour-blocked-sections-copper-platinum-black-silver.png` — ask: **hair colour only**

**CORRECTED, and the file was renamed** (fable-1079 §1). It was filed as
`hair-colour-multipanel-copper-blonde-black.png` and described as a multi-panel
job in three tones. Opened at working size it is **one photograph of one woman,
no panels**, and it holds **four** blocked tones — a bright orange-copper fringe
panel, a platinum-blonde panel beside it, near-black roots and lengths behind,
and a silver-white section on the far side.

**The difficulty is not the one first flagged.** *"Does the reader flatten it to
one word"* is a bar a completely wrong answer passes: *"copper, blonde and
black"* names every tone in the frame and describes an ombré, a balayage, any
head at all. **The colour words have to carry WHERE each tone sits.** That
constraint shaped the reader before it was written
(`UNIVERSAL_REFERENCE_ROAD_DESIGN.md` §9.2 and §9.5).

It is also the counterexample that kills the obvious panel rule: her hair hangs
down both sides of her face, so a hair mask over this frame is two components on
ONE head. Counting hair components cannot tell a composite from a centre part.

## 6. `tattoo-patchwork-torso-neck-continuation.png` — ask: **use all these tattoos, onto the neck**

A real photograph: a shirtless, heavily tattooed torso, cropped at the chin. A
barbed-wire band at the base of the neck with throat work above it (a "666" at
the chin line), chest and stomach pieces (a snake, a rose, crossed knives
reading THE / END, "DANCE MUSIC", "1996", an all-seeing eye, a death's-head
moth, two cherubs, a spider at the navel), and both sleeves worked.

**It carries both halves of the visible-extent promise in one specimen**
(founder ruling, fable-1081): with the package's crew tee on, the NECK work must
show above the collar, the chest and stomach work must NOT, and the sleeves show
what a short sleeve allows. `scripts/court-view-reference-disposable.mts` now
refuses to spend without a specimen that reaches bare skin, and this is that
specimen.

Three things it raises before any render is bought:

- **it is a PATCHWORK** — many distinct designs in one photograph, and his ask
  is to use all of them. On the interim road (reference plus words) it rides
  whole; the crop road must rule one-region-or-many deliberately.
- **containment** — the chin and jaw are in the frame, so the face-exclusion
  discipline applies to whatever is cut.
- **text** — several pieces are lettering, and engines render text unreliably.
  A court records what the text actually came out as rather than scoring the
  shapes and calling it a pass.

## What these files are, and what they are not

They are **third-party photographs the founder supplied as test material**, kept
in the repository because a corpus that exists on one laptop is not a corpus:
three documents and one court quote these filenames, and a fresh clone has to be
able to run them (ruled fable-1089 §3, on the precedent that
`docs/specs/references/` already holds his supplied frames throughout).

The repository is private. If that ever changes, the whole `references/`
directory is revisited as one decision rather than this folder alone.
