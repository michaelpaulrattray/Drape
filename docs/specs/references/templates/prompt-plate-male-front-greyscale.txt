INTENDED USE

A blank template plate for a tattoo "flash sheet" system. A customer's tattoo
design is later drawn onto this blank form, and that drawing becomes the
reference the studio carries. This plate is a CANDIDATE for a founder's taste
review. It must contain NO tattoo, NO design, NO ink and NO text of any kind.

WHY THIS PLATE EXISTS

The set currently has only a female form. The founder asked for a male one:
"also do you a male torso the one in th example is female." This is that plate.

THE REFERENCE IMAGE IS THE FAMILY STANDARD

The supplied image is the approved FEMALE FRONT plate, already amended to remove
the arms. Copy everything about its TREATMENT and change only the BODY.

COPY EXACTLY FROM THE REFERENCE

- GREYSCALE. Neutral grey form, no colour of any kind, no warm or cool tint.
- The same near-white background, flat and even, no gradient, no vignette, no
  floor line, no shadow cast on it.
- The same canvas proportions and the same framing: the form occupies the same
  share of the frame, sits at the same height, and is centred the same way.
- The same soft even studio light and the same matte, low-contrast shading. No
  glossy highlights, no harsh shadows, no rim light.
- THE SAME TERMINATIONS. The neck ends in a soft wide fade at the same height.
  The bottom of the torso ends in a soft fade at the same height. The arms are
  CUT OFF AT OR JUST BELOW THE SHOULDER: the shoulder and deltoid cap fully
  present and reading clearly as a shoulder, then dissolving into the background
  in the same soft gradual fade. Never a hard cut, never a sliced edge, never an
  end face, never a seam, never an outline or halo.
- ONE form, from ONE angle — the FRONT. Never a second view, a mirrored copy or
  a panel beside it.

CHANGE ONLY THIS: THE BODY IS MALE

- Unmistakably a male upper body: broader and squarer shoulders, a wider
  ribcage, a FLAT pectoral chest with no breast form whatsoever, a thicker neck
  base, and a straighter waist with much less inward taper than the reference.
- The musculature is gently indicated, not a bodybuilder: an ordinary,
  athletic-but-average adult male form, sculptural and anonymous.
- Keep the same overall height in frame as the reference, so the male and female
  plates read as a matched set when placed side by side.

STILL A MANNEQUIN, NOT A PERSON

No head, no face, no hair, no nipples, no navel, no body hair, no jewellery, no
clothing. Smooth, anonymous, sculptural.

Nothing else in the frame: no labels, no captions, no watermark, no logo, no
measurement marks, no border.
