INTENDED USE

A blank template plate for a tattoo "flash sheet" system. A customer's tattoo
design is later drawn onto this blank form, and that drawing becomes the
reference the studio carries. This plate is a CANDIDATE for a founder's taste
review. It must contain NO tattoo, NO design, NO ink and NO text of any kind.

THIS IS AN EDIT, NOT A NEW PICTURE.

  edit target — the supplied image: the approved greyscale female BACK plate.

The founder approved this plate's look and asked for exactly ONE amendment:

  "for the full torso upperbody front/back id the cut the arms off at or just
   below the shoulders"

THE ONE CHANGE

Remove the arms. The shoulder itself, the deltoid cap and the full width of the
shoulder line all REMAIN and must still read clearly as a shoulder. Immediately
below the deltoid cap, the arm dissolves into the white background in the SAME
soft, gradual fade already used at the neck and at the bottom of the torso in
this image.

- Never a hard cut, never a sliced edge, never a stump with a visible end face,
  never a seam, never an outline, never a shadow suggesting the arm continues.
- Do not narrow the shoulders or round them into balls. The silhouette from the
  neck across the shoulder must be unchanged from the target; only what hangs
  BELOW the deltoid cap goes.
- Do not widen, lengthen or reshape the torso to compensate. The upper back, the
  shoulder blades and the waist keep exactly the position, size and shading they
  have in the target.

WHY: those shoulders and the upper back are the surfaces a design is placed on,
and an arm hanging beside them is a second surface the image engine can put the
tattoo on by mistake.

EVERYTHING ELSE MUST BE IDENTICAL TO THE TARGET

- Same greyscale treatment. NO colour of any kind, no warm or cool tint: the
  form stays neutral grey and the background stays the same near-white.
- Same canvas size, same framing, same scale, same position of the form in the
  frame, same height of the neck fade, same height of the torso fade.
- Same soft even studio light, same matte low-contrast shading, no glossy
  highlights, no harsh shadows, no rim light.
- Still a MANNEQUIN, not a person: no head, no face, no hair, no nipples, no
  navel, no body hair, no jewellery, no clothing.
- ONE form, from ONE angle. Never add a second view, a mirrored copy or a panel
  beside it.
- Nothing else in the frame: no labels, no captions, no watermark, no logo, no
  measurement marks, no border.
