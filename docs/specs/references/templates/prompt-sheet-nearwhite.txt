INTENDED USE

A blank template asset for a tattoo "flash sheet" system. A customer's tattoo
design will later be drawn onto this blank form, and that drawing becomes the
reference the studio carries. This image is a CANDIDATE for a founder's taste
review of the template's look. It must contain NO tattoo, NO design, NO ink and
NO text of any kind.

WHAT TO PRODUCE

A single clean studio product image of a NEUTRAL, FEATURELESS GHOST MANNEQUIN of
a female upper body, shown as two panels side by side in one image:

  LEFT PANEL   the front of the upper body
  RIGHT PANEL  the back of the same upper body, same size, same lighting

Each panel shows: the base of the neck and throat, the collarbones and upper
chest, the shoulders, and both upper arms down to just above the elbow.

CRITICAL FORM RULES

- It is a MANNEQUIN, not a person: no head, no face, no hair, no nipples, no
  navel, no body hair, no jewellery, no clothing. Smooth, anonymous, sculptural.
- The form must be MATTE and LOW-CONTRAST — soft even studio light, gentle
  shading that reads the anatomy, no glossy highlights, no harsh shadows, no
  strong rim light.
- Every limb and the bottom of the torso must TERMINATE IN A SOFT FADE that
  dissolves into the background. Never a hard cut, never a sliced edge, never a
  visible stand, base, plinth or pole.
- The neck ends in a soft fade at the top. There is no head and no suggestion of
  one.
- One relaxed, symmetrical, canonical pose: arms hanging naturally slightly away
  from the body so the outer upper arm is fully visible, shoulders level, chest
  square to camera in the front panel.

SKIN AND BACKGROUND

- Mid-tone skin, a neutral medium beige-brown, matte, with no makeup, no
  tattoos, no freckles, no marks of any kind.
- Background: a flat, even, NEAR-WHITE studio background (a very light warm
  off-white, close to #F4F4F2 — clearly not pure white, clearly not grey),
  identical behind both panels, with no gradient banding, no vignette and no
  floor line.
- THE FADE MUST STILL DISAPPEAR INTO IT. The soft terminations above are the
  reason this background tone was chosen over a dark one: on a dark field a
  soft edge reads as a glow around the form. Against near-white the fades must
  vanish completely, with no halo, no outline and no visible edge where the
  form ends.

COMPOSITION

- Portrait or square orientation, high resolution, both panels the same scale,
  generous even margin around and between them.
- Nothing else in the frame. No labels, no captions, no watermark, no logo, no
  measurement marks, no border.
