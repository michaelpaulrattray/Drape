INTENDED USE

A blank template asset for a tattoo "flash sheet" system — the ARM sheet. A
tattoo that wraps around an arm must be drawn on several views of that same arm
on one sheet, so the design stays consistent from any angle (like a character
turnaround sheet). This image is a CANDIDATE for a founder's taste review of the
template. It must contain NO tattoo, NO design, NO ink and NO text of any kind.

WHAT TO PRODUCE

One image containing FOUR views of the SAME single neutral, featureless ghost
mannequin ARM, standing upright side by side in a row, evenly spaced:

  1  the OUTER side of the arm
  2  the FRONT of the arm
  3  the INNER side of the arm
  4  the BACK of the arm

It is one arm turned through four quarter rotations — a turnaround — not four
different arms.

WHAT EACH VIEW SHOWS

The arm from the top of the shoulder down to just below the elbow, including the
deltoid and the whole upper arm. No hand, no forearm below the elbow, no torso,
no shoulder blade, no neck.

CRITICAL RULES

- It is a MANNEQUIN limb, not a person's arm: no hair, no veins, no freckles, no
  moles, no jewellery, no clothing. Smooth, anonymous, sculptural, with just
  enough soft shading to read the muscle form.
- All four views are IDENTICAL in size, lighting and vertical alignment.
- MATTE and LOW-CONTRAST: soft even studio light, no gloss, no harsh shadow, no
  rim light.
- Both ends of every arm — the shoulder at the top and the arm at the bottom —
  TERMINATE IN A SOFT FADE that dissolves into the background. Never a hard cut,
  never a sliced edge, never a stand or plinth.
- Mid-tone skin, a neutral medium beige-brown, matte.

BACKGROUND AND COMPOSITION

- One flat, even NEAR-WHITE background across the whole image (a very light
  warm off-white, close to #F4F4F2 — clearly not pure white, clearly not grey),
  with no gradient and no vignette.
- THE FADES MUST DISAPPEAR INTO IT: no halo, no outline, no visible edge where
  each arm's soft termination ends.
- Landscape orientation, generous even margins, the four arms tidily aligned.
- No labels, no captions, no arrows, no numbers, no watermark, no logo, no
  borders.
