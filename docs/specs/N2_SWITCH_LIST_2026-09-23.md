## 2 · UPLOAD A CONCEPT

**Flag: `CASTING_CONCEPT_UPLOAD_SCOPE`.** Recommendation: **WIDEN with row 1 — but
this one has a question in it that is yours.**

**What a customer gets.** They drop in a picture of someone and get a written
description of the person back, in their own brief box, editable before they
spend a credit — instead of typing the whole description themselves.

**The evidence.** Built and flipped on your own word 2026-08-28 (*"Flip it."*).
The photograph is **never kept and never rendered**: the bytes ride the text call
inline and are dropped when it returns — no row, no table, no storage write
(`conceptDescribe.ts`, read at the code). What reaches the image engine is words,
so the product makes a *type* and never a likeness.

**What it costs.** House money, cents per upload — one text call with the picture
inline. No customer credits, no render.

⚠ **The question that is yours, and it is the only reason this is not a
formality.** The upload **rides whole to the text-model vendor**. While the
population is your own account that is your picture and your choice; at `all` it
is a stranger's photograph of a person going to a third party. The flag's own
catalogue entry says this re-opens explicitly on any widening, and it is a
product decision, not an engineering one.

**Its parent is row 1** — it cannot widen unless the author road does.

**Flag: `CASTING_BRIEF_FIDELITY_SCOPE`.** Recommendation: **WIDEN, at the same

**What a customer gets.** A detailed brief stops losing half of itself. Measured

⚠ **The order is load-bearing.** Widening this one *without* row 1 puts the lifted
