**A′ and T each pinned the region they showed and let the other go, 3 of 3, with

**S is the law's own prediction, and it half-lands.** Showing both regions in one
picture beats T on her face on 3 of 3 and beats A′ on her neck on 3 of 3 — the
only arm that is respectable in both columns. It is not as good in either column

## The limits

  measured on TONE in two regions. It is stated as a law because it predicted S
  before S ran, not because three faces make one.
- **fair-long resists every arm** (W 8.73, S 4.22). Whatever makes that face
  drift is not addressed by any carrier here, and no arm's median should be read
  without her in it.
- **A′'s identity column was measured in its own run**, against its own
  eye-floor; T's and S's are from this one. The three are comparable in R
  because the floors and frames are shared, and the E columns are comparable
  only in the loose sense.
- **The direction gate ran on T and S only.** A′ predates it.
