**A fact a customer types dies BY POSITION.** Not because it is a colour, not
because it is a tattoo, not because it is a cybernetic implant — because it sits
late in a sentence that has to fit a budget nobody told the customer about.

Measured, twice, this sitting:

uninspired"*, one line apart, on one sheet. **One mechanism, three complaints.**

## 3. What lands, in four pieces

### 3a. THE ANNOUNCED CAP — released, and replaced rather than deleted

```
from   "characterNotes": … Under 25 words.
to     "characterNotes": … Say every concrete, visible fact the brief states.
       Do not pad, do not repeat, and add nothing the brief does not contain.
```

Not merely deleted: the field's own restraint clauses (*write only what can be
SEEN*, no mood words, no brands, no numbers) all stay exactly as they are, and
this replaces the length sentence only. **The evidence that it is safe to
release is §2a's 8-of-8** — and the replacement text is the same text the two
courts actually drove, so what ships is what was measured.

### 3b. THE ENFORCED BOUND — `NOTES_MAX` 180 → 2000

```
✅ RULED  2000   CANNOT ration by construction: the brief itself is capped at
                 2000 characters (`castingV2.ts:1093`, `:1141`) and the notes
                 came in shorter than the brief on all 8 drives, so the bound is
                 the INPUT's own bound and can only ever catch a malfunction
rejected  1200   covers the longest real reply (957) with 25% headroom and would
                 have cut 0 of 8 — kept here with its reason rather than
                 deleted, because it is the number this seat recommended and the
                 countersign overturned it on a principle rather than a figure:
                 **a bound that is true by construction beats one that is true
                 by measurement**, and a measured bound is one unusual brief
                 away from being the defect again
rejected   957   the largest reply seen. A cap set AT the measured need makes
                 the model pay in content, which is the defect being repaired
```

✅ **Ruled 2000 (fable-1600).** The headroom reading's own finding is what makes
it safe: the notes came back SHORTER THAN THE BRIEF in 8 of 8 drives, so at 2000
the guard is not a content decision at all — it is a malfunction stop.

⚠ **AND THAT COSTS THE SUITE AN ARM, because a bound that never fires cannot
notice the thing it used to.** Ruled with it: **a reply longer than the brief it
summarises is a defect wearing length**, and the suite asserts it independently
of the cap. Measured 8/8 shorter; a summary longer than its input is a different
failure and must not hide behind a generous bound.

⚠ **The compression re-ask stays exactly as it is.** It fires on overflow, is
handed the model's own sentence, is length-checked, and runs once. At 2000 it
will almost never fire — and when it does, it will be answering a malfunction
rather than salvaging a routine sentence, which is what it was built for.

### 3c. THE LANE — `statedSkin`, per the stopped design's §5, unchanged

```

              fires — with the gate fixed, D-88 starts working on the six briefs
```

⚠ **Why the lane still ships when the budget is being fixed** — the obvious
objection, answered: a budget with room means the tone usually survives, and
*usually* is what this item is trying to stop being the answer. A lane is not
rationed at all, and the deference half is a separate defect (six of twelve
ordinary skin statements leave the engine authoring a skin character on top of
her word). **Neither half ships alone**: a lane that silences without speaking
is `statedHair`'s original defect, and widening deference alone is strictly
worse than today.

### 3d. THE COUNTERS — so this is the last time the loss is invisible

```
notesOverflow    unchanged, and it will now mean what it says
notesRationed    NEW. Logged when the raw reply is shorter than it would have
                 been — no. See the note below; this counter is NOT built
```

⚠ **There is no honest counter for the thing this build fixes, and pretending
otherwise would be the worse choice.** Mode B — a fact silently generalised out
of a summary that fits — is invisible by construction: the product never sees
the fact that was not said. The instrument that CAN see it is a driven corpus
comparing brief facts to compiled prompts, and that is
`_brief-fidelity-corpus-disposable.mts`, which is a court rather than a counter.
**So this build adds no new production counter, and says so rather than shipping
a number that would mean nothing.**

## 6. ⚠ The hardest question in this build, and it is not a technical one

**This change has no feature flag, and it reaches every account on the next
deploy.**

Every other capability in this program shipped dark behind a scope flag. This
one cannot, in any honest way: the announced cap and `NOTES_MAX` are properties
of ONE interpreter call that every roll makes, and a per-user branch would mean
two different products interpreting two different sets of briefs — with the
census, the corpus and every future reading having to ask which one it measured.

Three roads, and the recommendation is the second:

```
A  ship it to everyone     simplest, and the change is strictly more of what
                           the customer typed. The risk is §4's unmeasured
                           image side landing on every account at once
B  ✅ RULED — flag it      `CASTING_BRIEF_FIDELITY_SCOPE`, in the house shape,
                           parented on CASTING_V2_SCOPE. It is two constants
                           read per roll rather than a road, so the branch is
                           small and the measurement stays honest as long as
                           every reading records which side it drove
C  ship it dark and never  the worst of both: a flag that never widens is a
   widen                   second product nobody maintains
```

✅ **RULED B, WITH ITS LIFESPAN DECLARED SHORT (fable-1600), and the declaration
is part of the ruling rather than a hope.** The flag exists for ONE unknown —
the image side — so **it widens to `all` promptly after his gate rather than
living at `users:1` for weeks.** Two products interpreting two sets of briefs is
a cost this program pays briefly and on purpose; paid indefinitely it becomes
option C, which is the worst road on this list. **Every brief-fidelity reading
taken while the flag is narrow stamps which side it drove** — the run-label
lesson, which this campaign has already paid for once.

**B's real cost is stated rather than hidden:** the flag must be recorded in
`CLAUDE.md`'s enumeration and `productionFlagPositions.mts` (both are enforced),
and every future brief-fidelity reading must stamp which side of it ran — the
run-label lesson this campaign has already paid for once.

## 7. Open questions for the countersign

1. **`NOTES_MAX`: 1200 or 2000?** Recommendation: 1200 (§3b).
2. **Does the announced cap get the replacement sentence in §3a verbatim?** It
   is the exact text both courts drove; changing it at the countersign means the
   thing that ships is not the thing that was measured.
3. **Flag or no flag (§6)?** Recommendation: B.
4. **Does the lane ship in this commit or the next?** Recommendation: this one —
   it is the same population and the same court, and a second prompt change on a
   second tree is exactly the interleaving the one-at-a-time rule exists to
   prevent.
5. **Is stage 3 (160 credits, one roll) authorised as part of this package, or
   does it come back separately once the text stages pass?** Recommendation:
   separately, on his word, because it spends his credits and it is his gate.
6. **Does `Under 25 words` leave any other trace?** ⚠ Read at the code, and the
   answer is a sibling rather than a copy: `"role"` carries its own announced
   cap, *"under 12 words"*, and it is the OTHER field that reaches the image
   model. **This build does not touch it, and I am not claiming it is fine — I
   am claiming it is UNMEASURED.** No court in this campaign has counted whether
   a role is rationed the way a note is, and the reason to leave it alone is
   that changing two announced caps in one commit would make the court unable to
   say which one moved anything. Named here so the sweep is on the record rather
   than assumed, and filed as the next question rather than as a closed one.
   (Working law 7: a bug found once is a pattern until proven unique — this is
   the sweep, and it comes back UNKNOWN rather than CLEAN.)
