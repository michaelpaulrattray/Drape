## 0 · The feature, in product terms

Wherever there is a brief box, one small quiet glyph. Press it and the studio
takes the words in the box and writes back a new idea born from them — into the
box, visibly, editable, with Undo. Press again for another idea. Casting always
uses whatever is in the box. It costs nothing until you cast.

**The disappearing-technology gate, answered in this body:**

1. *What must the customer learn?* Nothing. There is no level to set, no mode
   to understand, no picker. One icon whose hover says "Re-imagine" and whose
   result appears in their own box, where they already know how to edit.
2. *What decision does it put in front of them?* One, with a full basis: keep
   what came back, edit it, or Undo — all judged by reading their own brief box.
   The Low/Max decision (offered today with no basis for making it) is deleted.
3. *Where does the technology show?* Nowhere. No engine name, no percentage,
   no "imagination level". A press that has nothing to offer says so in plain
   words in place; a wait is the glyph turning, not a stage name.

## 3 · The instruction — what the author becomes (his decisions 3–11, the correction, both courts)

The base is the #477 verbatim-discipline register (tight clauses, never
re-describing what it keeps). On top of it, his contract:

- **Locked when typed, and only these: sex, age, species.** Everything else
  the customer typed is the spark, not the cage. (Correction of 2026-09-05,
  settled by his own two rolled courts: rolls 244 vs 245 — his "10x better" —
  and 243 vs 246 — "much better". Named colours and materials ARE pieces.)
- **Read the register first, carry it as qualities.** A detailed brief has
  MORE register to keep, not less; identity nouns only for the two or three
  things that make the type the type; everything else written so eight faces
  answer it eight ways.
- **Never lighting, camera, framing, backdrop or scene** — a BAN in the
  instruction now, not a preference (today an authored lighting clause can
  override the house block's AUTHORITY line).
- **Starts with the person; no titles or names in the box; short by design.**
- **Heat never contradicts a LOCKED fact and never contradicts itself** (the
  reconciled #477 clause — a paragraph naming one material two ways is refused;
  changing the material consistently is the feature).
- **Deleted:** the FINISHED-seed "HEAT ONLY … more severe" mode; "lighting
  taste"; FACTS-STAY-in-full (it contradicts decision 3); `droppedFactIn` as a
  refusal (see below).

**Guards, kept and retired** (the card's "guards that existed only because the
rewrite was invisible"):

| guard | fate | why |
|---|---|---|
| `NEVER_WRITTEN` (pipeline notes, set words, "sternum") | **kept** | protects the roll, not the reader |
| house-sentence / `containsHouseSentence` | **kept** | the block stays code's, appended last |
| `FILTER_RULE` (provider safety) | **kept** | protects the roll |
| `skinContradictionIn` | **kept** | the block's own negatives still refuse the picture |
| `pieceNounIn` / `PIECE_NOUNS` | **kept** | qualities-never-pieces is now the whole instruction; the guard is its backstop |
| `ageContradictionIn` + stated-sex check | **kept, narrowed to the locked trio** | sex/age/species when typed still cannot move |
| `droppedFactIn` (every stated fact survives) | **retired** | its contract IS the pieces road his courts rejected; the result lands editable in the customer's own box, which is the new fidelity control — they read it |
| one-paragraph / allowance / stacked checks | **kept** | shape rules, unchanged |
| species lock | **new** | decision 4 names it; today nothing checks species |

The fallback road (twice-refused → the customer's words stand) becomes the
honest in-box line instead of a silent mode change.
