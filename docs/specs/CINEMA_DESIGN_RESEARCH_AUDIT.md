# Cinema design × research — the audit

**Founder-ordered 2026-10-12** ("review the full design process and ensure it truly supports the research"), after he caught the design process asserting research alignment that had not been checked. **The process failure is recorded first:** the first interactive mockups (the three-mode shell, then the one-road revision) were checked against the *rulings* (§16.10 of `12-cinema-full.md`) but not against the *research record* — and were presented as research-aligned anyway. This audit re-reads the record and checks every design element against it. Sources read in full for this audit: `research/zephyr-teardown/implications-for-drape.md`, `MAKERS-BRIEFS.md`, `narrative-and-continuity.md`; `docs/specs/COMPETITOR_WORKFLOW_ANALYSIS.md`, `SCRIPT_ROOM_DESIGN.md`, `FILM_ENGINE_DESIGN.md`; `research/cinedance-block-library/` (both files); §16.10.

The design under audit: the working prototype pair of 2026-10-12 (A = the ruled three-mode shell; B = the one-road revision the founder's eye preferred), plus the direction they imply. Verdicts: **ALIGNED** (research supports it), **WAS MISSING — FIXED** (research-ordained, dropped from the prototypes, now restored in B v3), **DELIBERATE DEVIATION** (founder-ruled against the research's grain, named), **OPEN** (needs the founder's word).

## 2 · Was missing — restored in prototype B v3 (2026-10-12)

1. **Stills before video — the spend gate.** COMPETITOR take-1, already *adopted* into the design pack (12b §H): "the first look becomes STILLS — the first frame of each approved shot, image-priced, judged on the contact sheet before any video is drawn." It is also ZEPHYR's layer 2 (2,157 shot-plate jobs — "the layer where the everyday-person promise lives") and the only mechanism that makes the median-5-tries economics affordable at §16.10's customer-chosen take counts. Both prototypes had gone straight to video. **Now:** each shot's drawer leads with "First look · 4 stills," video takes open from the picked frame, skippable.
2. **Notes filed as labelled chips.** `FILM_ENGINE` 5b level 1, the founder's own 2026-08-25 reshape: a note is one plain box, the engine files it into the right slot and shows the filing as a removable chip ("DON'T SHOW · looks at camera ×"). The prototype's note box saved invisibly. **Now:** chips with slot labels, removable.
3. **The escalation ladder.** `FILM_ENGINE` 5b: when takes keep missing — more takes first (rerolls are pure sampling, measured), then a note, then **fix the ingredient** ("the inverted-sheet lesson: when prompting fails, change the input"); the prompt editor deliberately last. **Now:** the ladder appears as quiet copy after two unkept takes.
4. **"Propose shots · free."** `SCRIPT_ROOM` §4 (the DP's coverage strip, beat-typed, approve/edit/delete) and the ruled free/paid line ("thinking free, rendering paid", founder 2026-09-02). **Now:** per scene, free, suggestions removable, the writer's own lines never touched.
5. **Dramaturgy chips.** `SCRIPT_ROOM` §2 and Adiliada's "a weak scene costs real money: you find out only after you've generated it." Advisory, never a gate. **Now:** "Worth a look · no turn in this scene yet" on thin scenes.
6. **Paste-a-script as a first-class door.** The founder's own main road (`SCRIPT_ROOM` gate 1: "you uploaded your script on entering the studio") beside tell-it-plain. **Now:** second button on the opening screen.

## 3 · Deliberate deviations — ruled, and named as such

- **"Assets first, then nothing moves" (Adiliada's law #1) vs §16.10 point 5 (approval never blocks; places invent freely).** The founder's ruling deliberately softens the pros' discipline for everyone but *identity*: an uncast character still gates the take (their law survives where drift hurts most — the face), while places and props mint from the writer's words and file on keep. Defensible because the engine, not the customer, owns plate consistency; recorded so nobody "fixes" the gate into a full assets-first gate later.
- **"Absorb the volume" (zephyr implication #4: buy takes in the backend, show a few good ones) vs §16.10 point 3 (customer-chosen take count starting at 1).** The founder ruled the customer's hand on the lever. The stills-first gate (§2.1 above) is what reconciles the economics; without it the ruling is expensive, which is why its omission was the audit's worst finding.
- **No four-tab shell (Script/Wall/Desk/Composer) and no three-mode shell.** `SCRIPT_ROOM` §8 recommended lenses over surfaces; §16.10 ruled three centre modes; the founder's eye on the working version (2026-10-12: "doesn't feel intuitive") moved the live direction to one road. The research's load-bearing fact — script → scene → shot → take → cut is **one derivation chain** — is honoured by both shapes; the chain is the invariant, the shell is taste, and his eye is king. **§16.10 point 1 needs amending on his word if B is picked** (see §4).

## 4 · Open — needs the founder's word

1. **The shell.** A (three modes, ruled) vs B (one road, his eye's current lean). If B: §16.10 point 1 is amended, quoted, and Yuna's frames re-briefed.
2. **The budget line while writing.** `SCRIPT_ROOM` §6 ("~74 shots · est. 2,900–4,400 cr"; "nobody discovers the cost after writing the epic") sits against §16.10 point 4 ("everything before approval… no prices on screen"). An *estimate footer* is not a price on a button, but the ruling's words say none — his call. The prototype shows none.
3. **Scene-level engine staging** as a filed ruling ("a scene can be one engine-staged take; keeping is the filing") — demonstrated in B on his instinct, not yet his confirmed word.
4. **Voice lane** unchanged: dashed everywhere until the voice feature; the research's dialogue-in-one-generation finding (27% of keepers) shapes the engine then.

## 5 · Engine-side obligations the UI design must not foreclose (for the build, from the record)

The compiler's craft lives behind the surfaces and is already specified: the 12-block CINEDANCE order; per-reference scopes derived from what each plate contains; failure-mode bans in the negative field with a positive prompt body; identity restated in words for *people*, never for marks (fable-1399); blocking diagrams and depth maps for multi-character/space-critical scenes (`Home_Scheme` — "the pattern to copy first"); state flips at story events; sacred pixels (the face never regenerated); the small-task, inversion-negatives, fail-conditions and count locks from `research/cinedance-block-library/`. Nothing in prototype B blocks any of these; the "How this was made" drawer is where their plain-words shadow surfaces.
