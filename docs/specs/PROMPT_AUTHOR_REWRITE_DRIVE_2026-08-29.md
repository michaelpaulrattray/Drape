## What the drive changed in the build, and it is worth more than the passes

Two defects were found by DRIVING it that no unit arm would have shown, and
both are declared here rather than quietly patched:

1. **The instruction contradicted FACTS STAY.** Roll 228's seed says
   *"doll-like porcelain android presence"*, and the first skin rule told the
   author not to write doll-like skin — so it hunted synonyms ("waxy"), was
   refused twice, and the customer LOST MAX and fell back to her own words. The
   rule now says *do not ADD* one, and keeps hers. On the re-drive that seed
   authored cleanly on the first attempt.
2. **The rewrite narrated its own obedience to the image engine** —
   *"the sense of manufactured texture the user specified"*. That is §5b defect
   3 (pipeline notes in the image prompt) in new clothes, and it passed every
   guard because no list held the phrase. `the user` is banned now, the
   no-notes rule says it in words, and the phrase was taken OUT of the author's
   own instruction, because a word in the ear is a word that gets echoed.
