## What happens today, driven

```
marks removal        REFUSED · uncatalogued  · "skin has no vacant phrase in the
                                               placement table"
ink removal          REFUSED · unnamedObject · "the placement table cannot name
                                               what that is"
facialHair removal   OK      · slot facial-hair          ← last night's slice
```

Two different refusals for two different reasons, and that is the whole map.

## marks — the slot has no KEY to hang a vacancy on

`marks` folds into the `skin` slot, and `skin` has **no question and no guard
kind** (its region is `face skin` for DISPLAY only — a face crop filed as her
skin is a partial wearing the name of the whole). The vacancy lookup is keyed by
`guardKind`, so for marks it is keyed by `null` and can never find anything.

**So marks needs more than a sentence.** It needs a kind id, and the honest one
is not "skin": one slot holds several kinds of mark, exactly as
`statedAccessories` holds earrings, glasses and a nose stud. *"Take the freckles
off"* and *"take the scar off"* are different absences of different sites.

The shape that follows the grain of the code:

```
1  a MARK KIND vocabulary — words → kind ("freckles", "scar", "birthmark",
   "mole"), the same shape `LANDMARK_OF_ACCESSORY` has for worn things and for
   the same reason: the code owns the matching, the model reads intent only
2  a vacancy phrase per kind, in the kind-keyed home slice (b) already built
3  the kind threaded to `repaintAsksFor` the way `accessoryKind` already is —
   one more override, not a new mechanism
4  the removal court, per kind, with the beard's four arms
```

**The declared shortcut, if it is wanted:** ship ONE kind first (`freckles` — the
founder's own specimen, and the one the marks work already measures) and let
every other mark refuse exactly as today. No regression, a real capability, and
the per-kind vocabulary named as the real source rather than skipped. If that is
taken, it must be said out loud in the report — a lesser path taken silently is
the violation, not the lesser path.

## ink — it has no slot at all, and that is FILED rather than missing

`ink`'s facet card says it plainly: *"OWED, not absent: ink is per placement and
its question comes from the placement rather than from a region table, so its
slots arrive with the tattoo studio and the flash-sheet path (D-138, roadmap
§3). Inventing a `tattoo` question here would ask a segmenter an open question
(D-213)."*

So ink's removal is not a phrase problem and cannot be fixed by one. It refuses
one door EARLIER than marks (`unnamedObject`), and it stays refused until the
studio gives a tattoo a slot. **Nothing in this slice should touch it**, and the
refusal it gives today is the honest one.

## What I recommend

1. **marks, one kind (`freckles`), declared as a first kind rather than the
   vocabulary** — 3 registrations' worth of work by V1's measure, plus a court
   at about $1.50.
2. **ink stays where it is**, with its refusal, until the tattoo studio.
3. And a rider worth deciding once rather than per kind: a mark removal is a
   SURFACE removal, and the surface lesson is already on the record — *a tan can
   neither be held nor removed*. Freckles are the same class of fact. So the
   marks court should carry the tan's own survival arm: **remove the freckles,
   then do an unrelated edit, and see whether they come back.** If they do, the
   capability is cosmetic and the slice reports SHORT rather than shipping.
