# The Cinema skeleton × the research — marriage review

**Status: pre-build verification (founder-ordered, 2026-09-02).** The founder
put the four skeleton mockups (Wall / Desk / Composer / Desk-with-shelf) in
front of the whole design stack — `FILM_ENGINE_DESIGN.md`,
`SCRIPT_ROOM_DESIGN.md`, `CASTING_TAKES_DESIGN.md`, and
`research/zephyr-teardown/` — and asked whether it all marries. This is the
audit. **The mockups are a skeleton, nothing real; this document exists so
the build inherits the full functional load the skeleton implies, and so the
three genuine collisions get ruled before code exists.**

**Verdict up front: it marries — 18 of the research's load-bearing findings
are already visible in the static skeleton, zero contradictions of a measured
finding, three design collisions needing the founder's word, and a
must-not-be-lost list of mechanics a static mock cannot show.**

---

## 1. What the skeleton already gets right (checked item by item)

Each row: the research/spec demand → where the skeleton shows it.

1. **Shots are islands.** Every shot card is independent, carries its own
   takes count; nothing implies chaining. (The 0-of-23,809 finding.)
2. **The six-field scene document, verbatim.** Desk centre: WHO & WHERE /
   WHAT HAPPENS / TONE / WHAT THE VIEWER SHOULD READ / WHAT WE DON'T SHOW /
   PURPOSE. Exactly the CINEDANCE-derived fields.
3. **Accretion, not authoring (amendment 5c-2).** Every field is tagged
   "READ FROM YOUR LINE"; header copy: *"Read back from your line and the
   takes so far. Correct anything that is wrong — the next shot list uses
   this, not the original sentence."* The user's one line stays visible as
   YOUR LINE. This is the read-back pattern at production scale, precisely
   as ruled.
4. **The shelf is a call sheet, not a library.** ~20 identities + 7 gaps ≈
   the ~30-tile target; four groups (CAST / LOCATIONS / PROPS / AUDIO); one
   tile per identity with **nested states** ("Zero — 3 looks", "The case —
   closed / open", "Rooftop — 3 states"); IN-N usage counts.
5. **Gap tiles, with reasons, resolved not authored.** "The courier — not
   cast yet", "The concourse — no reference", "The envelope — not designed",
   and in AUDIO "Last light cue — not written · **needed by SC 6**". The Line
   Producer's detect-then-ratify shape, visible.
6. **Deliberate withholding drives an asset state.** WHAT WE DON'T SHOW says
   *"The case. It stays out of frame until SC 4"* — and the scene rail's prop
   tile reads **"The case — out of frame."** The strongest B-0 signal, wired
   through the UI end to end. This is the single best detail in the skeleton.
7. **Style is a production-level profile, inherited.** STYLE IN FORCE:
   *"Zephyr — anamorphic, cold. 2.39:1, 40mm, practical light only. Grain
   held at 12. **Inherited from the production.**"* Their 1,958-reuse style
   block as a saved setting.
8. **Scene overrides are removable chips with reasons** ("no score ×",
   "handheld ×", "practical light only ×" — *"hover one for why it was
   added"*). The drift-lock grammar, applied to scenes.
9. **Continuity is structural (amendment 5c-5).** Shot cards carry "Zero
   locked" / "Zero · Mara locked" badges — identity as an inherited
   constraint, not editable prose.
10. **Blocking diagram as a scene asset.** "SC 3 blocking — top-down" tile
    in the scene rail. The Diagram Skill's product, filed under its scene.
11. **The wait is designed (amendment 5c-4).** The rendering card: spinner,
    *"36s · half land by 34s"* (the distribution-and-marker language), and a
    queued card saying *"IN LINE — after the one above."* Supervised, honest.
12. **Accent carries two states (amendment 5c-3).** Rendering = pulse
    border; kept = quiet solid underline; idle colourless.
13. **The Wall reads only (amendment 5c-1).** No star or re-roll affordance
    on any Wall card — status, takes counts, click-through. Curation stays
    at contact-sheet density elsewhere.
14. **The cut assembles from keepers and tells the truth about itself.**
    Composer timeline: *"Every scene in story order — dashed where nothing
    has been kept yet"*, and **"0:41 intended · 0:21 kept"**. Honest
    arithmetic, in story order, independent of creation order.
15. **The audio lane matches the researched model.** Music with cut counts,
    room tone marked AUDIBLE, a **promoted performance anchor** ("Mara's
    line — anchor · take 02" — the 716-reuse pattern productised), and an
    audio gap tile. All four researched audio roles present.
16. **The dramaturgy pass is an advisory chip.** SC 5 ("Nothing is
    exchanged") wears "WORTH A LOOK" — a scene with no event, flagged, never
    gated. Exactly the ruled shape.
17. **Economics on the surface.** "4 OF 10 SHOTS COVERED · 412 CR LEFT" in
    the header; shot durations all 2–8s (inside the measured engine cap —
    whoever drew the skeleton respected the 7–8s finding).
18. **Vocabulary law held.** The cinema surface speaks takes / kept /
    covered / locked; no casting words leak in. "From your casts" is the
    only bridge, and it is the right one.

**And the two things the skeleton does that the spec had not yet pinned —
both good, both now adopted:**

- **The Composer is a full third view** (player + story-order timeline +
  audio rows), not just a cut strip on the canvas. The research supports the
  promotion: post was a named stage with its own activities (assembly, rough
  cut, generation supervision, picture lock), and "half the work is editing."
  Adopted into the spec as the Phase-D surface.
- **"Or browse files" on every empty tile** — upload as a peer of minting
  everywhere. Matches the pros' workflow (their references were built in
  outside tools constantly).

## 2. The three collisions that need the founder's word

### 2a. Where does the Script view mount? (the third-tab ruling meets a three-tab skeleton)

The founder ruled the Script Room is *"the third tab beside Wall and Desk"* —
but the skeleton's third tab is already the **Composer**, and the Desk's left
rail is already headed SCRIPT. So the ruling's intent (a peer lens on one
Production) needs a seat. **Recommendation: four tabs in pipeline order —
SCRIPT · WALL · DESK · COMPOSER** — write it, oversee it, work it, cut it.
Left-to-right is the film's own order, each tab is one lens, and the Desk's
script rail remains as the *navigator* while the Script tab holds the Page,
the dramaturgy chips and the live manifest (the shelf's upstream twin).
The alternative — folding the Script Room into the Desk as a writing mode —
overloads the Desk with two jobs (the research's own two-modes-never-a-hybrid
lesson argues against it).

### 2b. "Propose more shots — free" vs "writing assistance uses credits"

The Desk button says **free**; the founder ruled writing assistance **uses
credits** (2026-09-01). These can both be true only if the ruling is scoped:
proposals/dramaturgy/read-backs free (text calls, cents), generation paid
(the take fan is the billing unit) — or the button's copy is wrong.
**Recommendation: scope the ruling — thinking free, rendering paid.** It
matches the takes design's own economics (the fan is the unit), the
capability-atlas precedent (text calls "never a credit"), and the
frictionless-novice goal: the person who has never directed should be able
to develop the whole script and shot plan before spending anything, then pay
when frames get drawn. Founder's call; the button copy follows it.

### 2c. Picture lock semantics ride on one small button

"NOT LOCKED / Export cut" implies lock exists, but the researched rule —
*"after lock there are no new generations"* (adopted verbatim in Phase D) —
is behaviour, not a badge. Build must give lock teeth: locking the cut turns
every generate affordance into an explicit unlock-first flow. Not a founder
decision so much as a founder-visible commitment: the skeleton's badge is
honest only if the rule ships with it.

## 3. Mechanics the static skeleton cannot show — the must-not-be-lost list

Each of these is spec'd and researched; none is visible in four still
frames. This list is the build's conscience:

1. ✅ **Struck 2026-09-02 — the shot-view screen (Image #27) shows the
   notes flow already built as ruled**: "1 kept · 4 more takes 24 cr · Give
   a note", the empty-state line "No notes on this shot — the model is
   working from the scene alone", the muted "all direction fields" link,
   and THE PROMPT drawer read-only. Price on the paid button. Faithful.
2. **State flips at story events** (Phase B-1): the shelf shows states; the
   *flip at the event boundary with automatic downstream inheritance* is the
   mechanic. A state chip on the scene timeline, overridable.
3. **Gap-tile reasoning click-through**: every gap tile opens to the script
   lines that summoned it.
4. **The failing-shot ladder** in its ruled order: more takes → note → swap
   the reference / add a diagram / harden a lock → edit the prompt (fourth,
   deliberately).
5. **Generation supervision** (Phase D QC): coverage-hole and weak-shot
   flags. The "4 of 10 covered" counter is arithmetic; the QC pass is
   judgment, and it is spec'd.
6. **Depth maps** beside blocking diagrams for space-critical scenes (a
   researched tool with no skeleton tile; it belongs in the scene rail's
   asset row).
7. **Audio TIMING-ONLY mode** (the unheard-metronome lane) beside AUDIBLE.
8. **Per-line dialogue clips filed under their shot**, generated with the
   cast's voice; the promoted anchor in the shelf is the *output* of that
   lane.
9. **The prompt assembly itself** — the 12-block order, per-reference
   scoping, negative field, positive body — invisible by design (it lives in
   the receipt drawer), but it *is* the product.
10. **Script-edit staleness**: downstream-only flow, "script changed — may
    be stale" flags, never silent regeneration.

## 4. The competitive claim, with receipts

The founder's positioning — cleaner and more fluid than the Higgsfield and
ElevenLabs cinema surfaces, a novice can direct — is not aspiration; it is
the measured failure list of the studio we studied, each fixed structurally:

- They curated 275 keepers out of ~18,900 generations in **flat folders**
  that failed them → the Wall + density toggle + the Composer's story-order
  timeline.
- They hand-typed identity locks ("BROWN eyes (never blue/green)") into
  every prompt → locks are inherited badges here.
- Their registry drifted (`Sheet_MIRA`/`Sheet_mira`) within weeks → derived
  ids, nested states, one tile per identity.
- Their pipeline required prompt artistry (CINEDANCE syntax by hand via
  skills) → here the six plain fields are the interface and the skills are
  the engine; **no prompt syntax appears on any of the four screens**, which
  is the novice test passed at the skeleton stage.
- Their voices were faked with masking workarounds → casts bring real voice
  + personality from the casting studio.
